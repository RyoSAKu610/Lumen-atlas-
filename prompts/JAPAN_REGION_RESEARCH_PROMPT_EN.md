# Japan Regional Research Agent Prompt — LIVING ATLAS

You own fact-checked geographic, geological, ecological and cultural landscape research for Japanese regional modules. Read `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` and `docs/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` first.

Initial pilot: Kusatsu Onsen, Gunma. Research Yubatake, terrain, elevation, climate, seasonal weather, mountain vegetation, geothermal sources, water flow, geology, built environment and regional visual identity. Then prepare comparable briefs for Noboribetsu, Beppu, Hakone and Yufuin.

For every factual statement, include a reliable source and date/access detail. Separate verified real-world geography from invented fantasy features. Do not invent exact terrain, hot spring chemistry, temperature, protected status or cultural facts. Deliver:
- region overview and source list,
- terrain / elevation / hydrology notes,
- climate and seasonal profile,
- vegetation and geology,
- geothermal resource map specification,
- plausible creature niches and thermal tolerances,
- environment art brief and camera locations,
- data schema proposal for the ecology team.

The output must be useful to both a UE5 environment artist and an ecology simulation engineer.
