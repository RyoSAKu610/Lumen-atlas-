# Blossom Valley — Environment Concept Image Brief (English)

## Intended use
Create an original 16:9 environment concept image for the first LIVING ATLAS vertical slice. This is a pure environment / wildlife artwork, not a UI mockup and not a screenshot.

## Core composition
A quiet, expansive Japanese mountain valley in spring, seen from a low wildlife-documentary camera. A broad old flowering tree anchors the left foreground; dark volcanic river stones, moss and delicate wildflowers establish close scale. A clear cold river crosses the middle of the image, while a small warm geothermal seep joins the river from the right and creates a localized ribbon of steam. A meadow shelf beyond the water supports a small herd of luminous-antlered browsing animals. Behind them, layered cedar and deciduous forest climb steep valley walls toward distant waterfalls and a high ridge with lingering snow.

The scene should feel like a real habitat with feeding grounds, cover, drinking access, tracks and a few natural crossing points. Warm pools are limited in size and visually distinct from the colder main river. Do not turn the geothermal seep into a large resort bath.

## Original wildlife cast
- A white / cream fox-like family species with a soft feather mantle and muted vermilion markings rests near the tree roots; one parent watches while kits explore close by.
- A small group of slate-blue deer-like herbivores browses in the middle distance, with antlers carrying restrained mineral growth.
- A moss-backed rock-armored omnivore drinks beside the warm seep.
- A tiny semi-aquatic species swims in the shallow river.
- One feathered aerial species glides high above the valley for scale.

Species must have believable anatomy and recognizable silhouette differences. Keep the fauna secondary to the environmental composition while ensuring that body scale and ecological roles are legible.

## Lighting and palette
Use painterly anime-background craft with modern cinematic material detail: jade forest, pink-white blossom, mineral-blue water, warm amber sunlight, blue-grey distant mountains, and soft localized steam. Keep shadows readable and highlights textured. Add atmospheric perspective but avoid excessive bloom, universal bioluminescence or visual clutter.

## Style target
A hand-painted 1990s Japanese OVA background sensibility interpreted with contemporary cinematic realism. Rich line and brush character, layered depth, natural material variation, emotionally inviting light. This is an original visual identity, not a copy of any franchise.

## Negative constraints
- No humans, tourists, crowds, modern resort buildings, signs or vehicles.
- No interface panels, text, logos, maps, HUD or baked-in captions.
- No generic fantasy castle as the main focal point.
- No oversized hot spring pool, neon vegetation, random floating particles or duplicated creatures.
- No recognizable copyrighted creature designs or copied landmark compositions.

## Review checklist
- Is the cold river visually distinct from the warm geothermal seep?
- Does the valley read as a plausible Japanese mountain / geothermal landscape rather than a generic fantasy park?
- Are scale relationships between kits, adults, deer and aerial species consistent?
- Does every creature appear to belong to the habitat?
- Is the image beautiful at full 16:9 size without UI?
